# AI Policy

1. Any use of AI must be disclosed. You must declare which model you used and the extent of AI assistance.
2. All issue, PR, discussion, and commit descriptions must be authored by humans, not AI.
3. Contributions must reflect your own voice and ideas, and should not "read" as being written by an AI.
4. This project requires a human in the loop who understands the work produced by AI and how it interacts with the greater system without AI assistance.
5. You must have a thorough discussion with the maintainers in an issue about the proposed design and change, and obtain their approval before opening a PR. We will close any PRs that we believe were created without meaningful involvement.
6. Any AI-generated code must be reviewed, tested, and simplified by a human before publishing.
7. Any AI products used must explicitly state they do not assert copyright over the work.
